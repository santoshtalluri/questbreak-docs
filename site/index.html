Pillar 01

Mastery Before Play

The break unlocks at the accuracy threshold with a hint cap. No currency, level, or standing can substitute.

Pillar 02

Earned Advantage

Power-ups earned while learning are spent in the arcade. Effort is felt inside the fun, not merely accounted for.

Pillar 03

Progress Is Never Lost

XP and cards are monotonic. Flow decays one tier, never to zero. Streaks have silent grace days.

Pillar 04

Interest Is Material

Five themes change the context of every question and never its rigor. The wrapper never simplifies.
